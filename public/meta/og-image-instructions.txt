OPEN GRAPH IMAGE INSTRUCTIONS

Place your default social media sharing image here named "og-image.jpg"

Recommended specifications:
- Size: 1200 × 630 pixels
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Max file size: Under 1MB

Design tips:
1. Include your name/logo prominently
2. Use a clean, minimalist design matching your website aesthetic
3. Consider adding a tagline or brief description
4. Use consistent colors from your website
5. Ensure text is large enough to be readable as a thumbnail

For individual blog posts, create images with the same specs and place them at:
/public/blog/[post-slug].JPG

Example: For a blog post at /blog/eating, the image would be at /public/blog/eating.JPG
